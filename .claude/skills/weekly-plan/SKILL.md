# Weekly plan

1. Using google-calendar MCP tools, list the next 10 days of events across ALL calendars — especially the school calendar (e.g. **ManageBac**: deadlines, tests, presentations) and the primary calendar.
2. Read `TO DO.md` (owner's daily rhythm, e.g. fixed study blocks), `.claude/memory/memory.md` (weak spots, active projects), and note upcoming items like presentations or coursework milestones.
3. Draft the week: **append** to `TO DO.md` a section `## Week of <Monday date> — Jarvis plan` with a day-by-day table (Day | Blocks | Why). Rules: deadline-driven first (backwards-plan from ManageBac due dates), keep his existing daily rhythm, place weak-spot revision in spaced-repetition slots (1d/3d/7d), one rest block per day, nothing before 07:00 or after 22:30.
4. Create calendar events for at most the 4 most critical study blocks, titled `Study: <topic> (Jarvis)` — skip any that conflict with existing events.
5. Never delete or rewrite existing TO DO content — additive only. End with a 3-sentence spoken-style summary of the week's shape.
